# MAYUR v1.4.2 — Complete MAYUR Rebrand, 3D 360° Virtual Pet & Refined Home Recommendations

## What's New

### Complete MAYUR Rebrand — *Where Sound Becomes Art.*
- **New Brand Identity**: Rebranded the entire music experience from SONORA to **MAYUR**, featuring the official tagline **"Where Sound Becomes Art."** on the splash screen, onboarding, and About section.
- **Official MAYUR Artwork & Icons**: Added the new golden peacock and vinyl record adaptive launcher icon, legacy mipmap icons, splash emblem, and updated branding consistently across Home, Music Player, Mini-Player, Online Music, Online Video, Profile, Settings, About, Notifications, and App Updates.
- **Renamed Branded Features**: Updated **MAYUR Mix**, **MAYUR Recommendations**, **MAYUR Sound Lab**, and **MAYUR Radio**, while keeping third-party music providers unchanged and preserving full upgrade compatibility with your existing library, playlists, and preferences.

### Interactive 3D 360° MAYUR Virtual Pet on Home Screen
- **3D Peacock Mascot Redesign**: Replaced the floating Your Mix overlay controls on the Home screen with the interactive **3D MAYUR Virtual Pet**, crafted with fluffy blue feathers, expressive turquoise eyes, golden crown feathers, emerald-cyan-gold tail plumage, black-and-gold MAYUR headphones, and a glowing audio waveform jacket emblem. Your Mix playlists remain fully accessible through the music sections.
- **Full 360° Movement & Physics**: Drag horizontally or vertically to smoothly rotate MAYUR a full 360° with natural inertia, independent head tracking, blinking, breathing, hops, and realistic tail feather follow-through.
- **Playful Touch & Music Reactions**: Tap the head, body, headphones, or tail—or long press—for expressive reactions, and watch MAYUR bob, glow, and dance in sync with your active music playback.

### Refined Home Screen Recommendations
- **MAYUR Recommendations**: Renamed the Home recommendation section from **MAYUR Recommend** to **MAYUR Recommendations**.
- **Cleaner Full-Width Header**: Removed the Refresh button and its reserved layout space next to the Home recommendation title so the heading and description span the full available width cleanly above the music cards, while keeping refresh controls intact in other sections and Settings.

### Previously Included
- **v1.4.1**: 3-step Personalized Music Taste onboarding & Profile editor, video-only Android System PiP fix, refined in-app floating PiP, and YouTube Trending discovery fallback reliability.
- **v1.4.0**: In-app floating video PiP, personalized recommendation engine, dedicated **Settings → Online Music** controls, and 60 FPS+ startup optimizations.
- **v1.3.7 & v1.3.6**: Pan-India Festival Atmosphere Engine and privacy-first Open-Meteo Weather Ambient Effects with zero GPS or location permissions.
